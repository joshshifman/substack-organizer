# Privacy policy

_Last updated: September 27, 2026_

This policy covers the Subscription Organizer for Substack browser extension ("the extension"). The extension is an independent project. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Substack.

## Summary

The extension runs entirely in your browser. It reads your Substack subscriptions so you can sort them into folders, and it never sends your data to the developer or anyone else. There is no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What the extension accesses

When you open the extension and choose **Continue** on its first-run notice, it uses your existing Substack login in Chrome to request, from substack.com:

- **Your subscriptions:** the publications you subscribe to, including subscriptions you've made private, whether each is paid or free, and each publication's name, logo, description, web address and author.
- **Recent posts** from those publications: titles, subtitles, dates, links, cover images and whether a post is for paid subscribers.

Your browser attaches your Substack login cookies to these requests, as it does when you visit substack.com. The extension cannot read, copy or store those cookies.

The extension does not read your email address, payment details, messages or reading history, and it does not access any website other than substack.com. Publication logos and post images are loaded from Substack's image server (substackcdn.com).

## What the extension stores

- **Cached Substack data** (your subscriptions and recent posts) in Chrome's local extension storage on your computer, so the extension opens quickly. Cached posts are replaced as they're refreshed and deleted after a week, or as soon as you unsubscribe from a publication.
- **Your folders and pins:** folder names, the order of your folders, and the ID numbers of the publications in each folder and of the ones you've pinned. These are saved in Chrome's sync storage. If you use Chrome Sync, Google stores this data in your Google account and syncs it to your other computers, under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The developer never receives it.
- **Whether you've accepted the first-run notice.**

## Sharing

The extension does not sell, transfer or share your data with anyone. The only network requests it makes go to Substack (substack.com and substackcdn.com), to fetch the data described above. Chrome Sync, if you have it turned on, is provided by Google.

The use of information received from Substack follows the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including its Limited Use requirements: the data is used only to show you your own subscriptions and posts and to let you organize them.

## Your choices

- **Clear cache** in the extension's sidebar deletes all cached Substack data. Your folders and pins are kept.
- **Export folders** downloads a copy of your folders and pins as a file on your computer.
- Deleting a folder removes it everywhere it's synced.
- **Uninstalling the extension** deletes its cached Substack data from your computer. To remove your folders and pins from Chrome Sync as well, delete your folders and unpin publications before uninstalling, or [clear your Chrome sync data](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/185277) in your Google account.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Changes to what data the extension accesses will also be described in the extension's release notes.
